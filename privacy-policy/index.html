Privacy Policy · Last updated: October 11, 2026

1. Overview

AniWorthy (“the App”) is a free, ad-free, fully offline anime trivia and quiz app for Android, published by codex-zubair (“we”, “us”). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App does and does not collect when you use it. By using AniWorthy you agree to this policy.

In one sentence: AniWorthy collects no personal data, contains no advertising or analytics, requests no permissions, and never connects to the internet.

2. Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data.

  • No account, sign-in, or registration is required.
  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contacts are collected.
  • No location data is collected.
  • No device identifiers, advertising IDs, or IP addresses are collected.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking tools are used.
  • We do not know which quizzes you take or how you score.

3. Internet Access

AniWorthy does not request or use the Android INTERNET permission. The App is technically incapable of connecting to the internet. All quiz questions and facts are bundled inside the App; nothing is downloaded, streamed, uploaded or synced.

4. Permissions

AniWorthy requests no Android permissions at all. It does not access the camera, microphone, location, storage, contacts, phone, SMS, or network. The only optional interaction with an external app is opening the GitHub or Privacy Policy links on the About screen, which opens your web browser only when you explicitly tap them.

5. Data Stored on Your Device

AniWorthy keeps a small amount of data locally so scores persist between sessions:

  • Your best score for each category and overall.
  • How many quizzes you have played, and your best answer streak.

All of this is stored in the App's private storage on your device using the operating system's preferences store. It is never uploaded, synced, or shared. Clearing it in the App's Settings screen, or uninstalling the App, permanently deletes it.

6. Third Parties

The App contains no third-party advertising, no third-party analytics, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The App uses the open-source url_launcher plugin solely to open links you explicitly tap. No data is shared with anyone.

7. Children's Privacy

AniWorthy is a general-audience trivia game suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it does not knowingly collect information from children or anyone else.

8. Data Security

Since no data leaves your device and none is transmitted, there is no data for us to secure or for a third party to breach. Your scores remain under your control on your own device.

9. Content

AniWorthy's trivia questions and facts are original, factual, and written by the developer. The App contains no video, audio, manga or other copyrighted media, and no verbatim quotations, dialogue or lyrics.

10. Open Source

AniWorthy's complete source code is public at github.com/codex-zubair/ani-worthy. Anyone may inspect the code to verify these privacy claims.

11. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be included in a future app update and published on this page.
