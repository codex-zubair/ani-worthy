--- Full description (max 4000) ---
AniWorthy is a fast, friendly anime trivia quiz that works completely
offline. Answer questions about studios, series, characters, creators and
the words every fan knows. No internet, no ads, no accounts — just you and
your anime knowledge.

Think you know your anime? Play a 10-question round and find out. Every
answer comes with a short explanation, so you learn something new even when
you are wrong.

HOW IT WORKS
- Choose a category, or let AniWorthy mix them all together.
- Answer 10 multiple-choice questions.
- Get instant feedback and a short fact after every answer.
- See your score, your grade and a full review of the round.
- Your best score for each category is saved on your device.

CATEGORIES
- Studios — who animated what, from ufotable to Studio Ghibli.
- Series — iconic shows, movies, years and episode counts.
- Characters — heroes, villains and sidekicks.
- Creators — the mangaka and directors behind the classics.
- General — the terms and traditions every fan should know.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- No internet permission at all — the app cannot go online.
- Zero Android permissions requested.
- No ads, no analytics, no trackers.
- All questions are bundled inside the app.
- Best scores are stored only in the app's private storage on your device
  and can be cleared any time from the Settings screen.

100% ORIGINAL CONTENT
Every question and fact is original and factual. AniWorthy contains no
video, audio, manga or other copyrighted media, and no verbatim quotations,
dialogue or lyrics.

Open source
AniWorthy's source code is public at
https://github.com/codex-zubair/ani-worthy

Built by codex-zubair.

AniWorthy declares NO Android permissions. It explicitly REMOVES the network
permissions so the app cannot go online:

AD_ID permission. The Play Console will not prompt for permission
declarations, and the app qualifies for the "No data collected" label.
