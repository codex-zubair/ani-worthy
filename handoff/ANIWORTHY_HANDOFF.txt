3. KEY FACTS / DECISIONS
========================================================================
- Name: AniWorthy (Play/App Store search showed no exact match; a formal
  trademark check is still advisable before publishing).
- Package: com.anivala.watchanimetv (client requirement; permanent).
- Content: 100% original & factual (studios, years, creators, terms).
  NO verbatim quotes/lyrics/media — deliberate, to avoid copyright issues.
- No permissions. No INTERNET. No ads, no analytics.
- Local storage uses shared_preferences only (best scores + play count).
- Scope v1.0: single-player offline quiz. 5 categories x 20+ questions,
  plus 18 "did you know" facts.
- Signing keystore is git-ignored; passwords in android/key.properties
  (also git-ignored). KEEP THE .jks SAFE.
